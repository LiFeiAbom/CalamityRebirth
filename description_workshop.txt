灾厄重生 / Calamity Rebirth
将旧版灾厄中具有代表性的内容重新带回现版本，并针对现代灾厄、经典1.4.2.101与CalRD分别适配配方和功能。
本模组不是旧版灾厄的完整复刻，而是按已实现内容逐步扩展的个人移植项目。部分属性、价格、稀有度、配方与视觉效果经过重新平衡或兼容调整。

【English】
Calamity Rebirth
Returns selected legacy Calamity content to current tModLoader versions with separate adaptation for modern Calamity, Classic 1.4.2.101, and CalRD.
This is not a complete restoration of legacy Calamity. It is a personal project expanded one implemented feature at a time. Some stats, prices, rarities, recipes, and visuals have been rebalanced or adapted for compatibility.
Current version: 0.1.2.6
[Currently Implemented Content]
· 15 buff potions: Yharim's Stimulants, Abyssal Elixir, Bounding Potion, Cadence Potion, Calcium Potion, Ceaseless Hunger Potion, Crumbling Potion, Holy Wrath Potion, Photosynthesis Potion, Profaned Rage Potion, Revivify Potion, Shattering Potion, Soaring Potion, Titan Scale Potion, Triumph Potion
· 7 food and drink potions: Bloody Mary, Caribbean Rum, Moonshine, Moscow Mule, Rum, Tequila Sunrise, Vodka
· Permanent upgrade: Celestial Onion, which permanently unlocks an additional accessory slot
· Five-class emblem EX accessories: Warrior Emblem EX, Fire Gauntlet EX, Ranger Emblem EX, Sniper Scope EX, Sorcerer Emblem EX, Celestial Emblem EX, Summoner Emblem EX, Papyrus Scarab EX, Rogue Emblem EX, Crystal Skull
· Advanced accessory chains: Elemental Gauntlet, Daedalus Emblem and Elemental Quiver, Ethereal Talisman line, Statis summoner line, rogue mirror stealth chain, Nanotech, Nebulous Core, the complete Heart of the Elements chain, the full Angel Treads to Seraph Tracers boot chain, and the Frost Barrier and Psychotic Amulet prerequisites for the Auric Tesla set
· Tarragon, Bloodflare, Silva, God Slayer, and final Auric Tesla armor series: five class helmets, breastplates, leggings, complete set bonuses, and recipes for all three supported Calamity branches
· Blood God Core series: Core of the Blood God, Bloodflare Core, Blood Pact, Flesh Totem, Bloody Worm Scarf, Bloody Worm Tooth
· Melee weapon: Ataraxia, including main and side projectiles, needles, two explosion stages, petal particles, and Shadowflame
· Custom debuffs: Burning III, Daybreak II, Electrified II, Shadowflame II, and Alcohol Poisoning
· Drinks use an independent alcohol level; exceeding level three triggers Alcohol Poisoning
· Holy Wrath and the Crumbling/Shattering potions can inflict Calamity holy flames or Armor Crunch on hit
· Custom rarities and a unified pricing system: Warrior EX, Ranger EX, Sorcerer EX, Summoner EX, Rogue EX, Burnished Auric, Cosmic Purple, Turquoise, and Pure Green
· Optional vanilla nerf reverts for classic Calamity branches
The author is a beginner programmer. This is a personal hobby project with no development team.

[Requirements and Compatibility]
This mod uses soft dependencies and supports:
· CalamityMod (modern version)
· CalamityModClassicPreTrailer 1.4.2.101
· CalRD 1.4.5.7
Enable only one Calamity branch at a time. At least one supported branch is required for all recipes, class stats, and advanced compatibility features.
Without a supported branch, base items may still load, but some recipes and features will be unavailable.
[Current Limitations]
· This version includes only implemented content and is not a complete legacy restoration.
· Multiplayer, PvP, alcohol levels, treasure bag conditions, three-branch recipes, and several damage-over-time effects still require ongoing testing.
· Some modern Calamity rarities and high-tier materials are implemented locally to keep the mod loadable without Calamity installed.
