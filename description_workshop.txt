灾厄重生 / Calamity Rebirth
将旧版灾厄中具有代表性的内容带回现版本的个人移植项目。
当前版本：0.1.2.7
【内容概览】
· 五套终局盔甲：龙蒿、血炎、始源林海、弑神者与古圣金源，包含五职业头部与完整套装效果。
· 五职业EX饰品、元素之握、血神核心、斯塔提斯召唤链、盗贼镜片潜行链、纳米技术、星云核心、元灵之心与天使之靴至炽天使翼靴链。
· 22种药水与酒类、天界洋葱，以及多种自定义减益、稀有度和价格体系。
· 可选的原版削弱回调，可恢复各灾厄分支对部分原版内容的调整。
【兼容与说明】
适配 CalamityMod、CalamityModClassicPreTrailer 1.4.2.101 与 CalRD 1.4.5.7，建议一次只启用一个灾厄分支。

为避免代码与美术素材版权纠纷，本模组明确不回归、不引进任何武器，也不包含武器贴图、专属弹幕或音效。

[English]
Calamity Rebirth
Returns selected legacy Calamity content to current tModLoader versions as a personal adaptation project.
Current version: 0.1.2.7
[Highlights]
· Five endgame armor sets: Tarragon, Bloodflare, Silva, God Slayer, and Auric Tesla, with helmets for all five classes and complete set bonuses.
· Class emblem EX accessories, Elemental Gauntlet, Blood God Core, the Statis summoner line, rogue stealth mirrors, Nanotech, Nebulous Core, Heart of the Elements, and the Angel Treads to Seraph Tracers chain.
· 22 potions and drinks, the Celestial Onion, custom debuffs, rarities, and pricing.
· Optional vanilla nerf reverts for supported Calamity branches.
[Compatibility and Notes]
Supports CalamityMod, CalamityModClassicPreTrailer 1.4.2.101, and CalRD 1.4.5.7. Enable only one Calamity branch at a time.
This is a personal hobby project, not a complete legacy restoration. Some stats, prices, rarities, recipes, and visuals have been rebalanced or adapted for compatibility.
To avoid code and artwork licensing disputes, this mod does not restore or introduce weapons and contains no weapon sprites, exclusive weapon projectiles, or weapon sounds.
When reporting an issue, include the mod version, Calamity branch, single-player/multiplayer/PvP environment, reproduction steps, and client logs.
